**The amendment, all of its parts:**
1. Per-workload prompt numbers exclude the chat-cache save and are taken on the engine clock.
2. The overall screen rule (g16's geometric mean) stays on the client clock minus the save, at -1 %.
3. The save is judged as its own line, against its own noise, with its two-level pattern taken into account.
4. The outside-engine INFO flag. It never changes a verdict.
   - **Outside-engine time per request:** client TTFT minus the engine's prompt ms. The save is inside the engine
     figure, so it cancels.
   - **Spread:** the larger of B's and P's half-ranges over their passes.
   - **Flag:** "outside-engine time worse in every pass - for review (INFO)" when each of P's passes is above B's mean
     by more than that spread.
   - It is in `g17.outside_flags` and is reported on the per-workload no-loss line, so it shows wherever that line
     appears (port0211, basalt, ud31 and q4 rules). confirm0211_rules.py reports it over the starts for R2 / R3.

**What it does:**
- **Per-workload prompt** (Karl 11-03's no-loss rule, and g16's "no workload more than 3 % below"): prompt tokens /
  (the engine's prompt ms minus the `chat cache: parked ... in N ms` logged just before that request).
- **Aggregate screen prompt** (g16's geometric-mean rule): the client clock minus the same save, i.e. prompt tokens /
  (TTFT minus the save).
- **INFO:** per-workload client-minus-save losses beyond their noise are listed, and the outside-engine flag (part 4)
  marks a workload whose server-side time is worse in every pass.
- **Strict pairing:** each request is matched to its engine-log line by prompt tokens. A record that cannot be matched
  makes the screen NOT DECIDED; it is never judged on fewer passes.

**The chat-cache save line** (`g17.save_rule`, one per screen, judged on its own):
- **Stored band:** `PARK_REF_1107` is built from the 872 stored r22/r23 saves. Only the 1238 MiB save has two levels
  (102-113 / 123-137 ms); every other size has one.
- **Save index:** each save is divided by the stored median of its level, using the sizes whose levels are 20 ms or
  more. The index is the geometric mean per pass, then over the passes.
- **PASS / FAIL:** FAIL when the arm's index is above the base's by more than the larger pass spread (at least 2 %),
  or when the arm has saves above the stored band (z > 6 over the top level) in 2 or more passes and more of them than
  the base. The level mix (fast / slow) is INFO and never judged.
- **A size with no stored band** (a new model's): it is compared with the base arm's median in that screen. A shift
  there is NOT DECIDED, never PASS or FAIL, because without a band a real slowdown cannot be told apart from a
  two-level draw.
- **NOT JUDGED (INFO):** a screen without saves, e.g. chat cache off.
